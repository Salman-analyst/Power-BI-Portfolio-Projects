🍽️ Restaurant Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

📊 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of restaurant sales performance across FY2024 and FY2025. It helps identify key revenue trends, site-level performance, menu insights, and discount impact across four UK restaurant locations. The dashboard is built on 1.2M+ transactional records and uses a Financial Calendar for accurate period-based analysis.

📈 Key Insights & Metrics
🔁 Year-on-Year Performance

Net Sales declined 4.3% from FY2024 (£6.53M) to FY2025 (£6.25M)
Transactions dropped 7.5% and Quantity Sold fell 9.3%
Discount Rate increased from 5.66% to 6.78% — discounting grew while revenue fell

📅 Financial Calendar — Period & Week Trends

P6 (peak summer period) dropped 9.6% in FY2025 — the sharpest single-period decline
Week 21 FY2025 was the strongest week, up 24.1% vs the same week in FY2024
Weeks 23–26 consistently underperformed FY2024 levels

📍 Site-Level Performance

London leads with 32.6% of total net revenue (£4.2M combined)
Birmingham recorded the steepest decline at 6.4% — above the group average
All four sites declined, confirming a company-wide trend rather than a single location issue

🍜 Menu Analysis

Creamy Mushroom Curry is the top item at £811K — over 6% of total net revenue
Sides group leads all menu categories at £2.82M
Food accounts for 89.5% of net sales; Beverages at 10.5%

💰 Discount & Transaction Analysis

Total discounts given: £846,302 across both years
48.58% of transaction lines were fully discounted (complimentary items, modifiers)
Manchester and Liverpool carry the highest discount rates by site

📆 Day of Week Trends

Sunday is the strongest trading day — 24.1% of weekly net sales
Weekend (Sat + Sun) accounts for 40.8% of total weekly revenue
Tuesday is the weakest day at 9.5%

🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

Power BI — Dashboard development & DAX measures
Power Query — Data cleaning, merging 5 tables, removing 497K null rows
DAX — YoY Growth %, Discount Rate %, Net Sales, Avg Transaction Value
Financial Calendar — Period and week-based time analysis (P5, P6, P7)
Excel — Initial data exploration

🔗 Data Source
The dataset contains transactional restaurant sales data across 5 tables — Sales (2024 & 2025), Menu, Site, and Financial Calendar — covering May to July in both financial years.

⚠️ Note: Original client data is confidential. The dataset included in this repository has been used with permission for portfolio purposes.

🚀 How to Use

Download the .pbix and .xlsx files.
Open Resturant_Data_Analysis.pbix in Power BI Desktop.
Ensure the dataset (Resturant_Data.xlsx) is properly linked via Power Query.
Explore the 4 dashboard pages — Overview, Menu & Site, Trends & Discount, Decomposition.
Use slicers (Year, Site, Menu Family) to filter and drill into specific segments.
